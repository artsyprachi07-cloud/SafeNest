🏠 Welcome to SafeNest

SafeNest is a student science-project prototype designed to encourage safety awareness, responsible reporting and community participation.

🌱 Our Idea

Many everyday problems remain unnoticed because people don't know where or how to report them.

SafeNest brings awareness, reporting and useful information together in one simple platform.

✨ What can you do?

📝 Report a civic problem

📊 View a sample community dashboard

🧠 Test your safety knowledge

📚 Use study tools

🚨 Find basic emergency information

📝 Report a Problem

Describe a civic or public-safety problem that needs attention.

✅ Thank you! Your report has been recorded in this prototype.

⚠️ Important

SafeNest is a student project and is not an official government complaint portal.

Do not share passwords, OTPs or other sensitive personal information.
